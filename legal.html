Terms of Service

1. The service

Cloud Cost Auditor analyzes the cloud bill text you paste (AWS, GCP or Azure) using artificial intelligence and returns estimated areas of wasted spend with suggested actions. It does not access your cloud accounts.

2. Estimates only

Results are AI-generated estimates based only on the text you provide. They may be incomplete or wrong. They are not financial, engineering or professional advice. You are responsible for verifying any finding before changing your infrastructure, and we are not liable for outages, data loss or costs arising from changes you make.

3. Fees

(a) Audit fee: a one-time fee of CA$149.00, paid in advance through Stripe. (b) Success fee: after you make changes, you may paste a newer bill so we can verify realized savings. We compare it with your audit and count only savings that bill supports, never more than the original estimate. The success fee is 10% of the verified monthly savings, charged once per audit. (c) Fees are in Canadian dollars unless the success fee is shown in your bill's currency. If we become required to collect sales taxes, they will be shown at checkout.

4. Saved card and approval

When you pay, you authorize us to have Stripe save your card for the success fee. We will charge the success fee only after you review and approve the exact amount on the page. Fees below a small minimum (currently 5.00) are not charged. You may ask us to remove your saved card at any time.

Provide bill text you have the right to share. Remove account IDs, names, credentials, access keys and other personal or secret information before pasting. Provide an honest and unaltered newer bill for verification. Do not misuse, overload or attempt to break the service.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

(a) The bill text you paste for the audit and, later, any newer bill text you paste for verification. (b) Your email address and payment details, collected by Stripe at checkout. We do not receive or store your full card number. (c) Technical identifiers such as your Stripe customer and payment references and the audit results we generate for you.

How we use it

To generate your audit, process payment, verify savings, charge an approved success fee, respond to support requests, prevent fraud and keep required business and tax records. We do not sell your information.

Bill text

The text of your original bill is deleted from our database once your audit has been generated. The text of a newer bill is used to verify savings and is not stored. We keep the audit results (findings and amounts, without your pasted bill text) and fee records.

Service providers

We use Stripe (payments), Google (the Gemini API, which receives your bill text to produce the analysis), Render (hosting) and Neon (database). Your information may be processed on servers outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of those places.

Retention

We keep audit results and payment records for as long as needed to provide the service, handle disputes and meet legal and accounting requirements, and then delete them.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) you may ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent to saving your card, by emailing info@fi-computing.com. If you have a complaint we cannot resolve, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
